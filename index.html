Privacy & Data Support & FAQ
🛡️

Local-First Architecture

Your phrase libraries, review history, and core repetition routines stay on your device. Optional iCloud backups, voice pack downloads, and AI translation assistance access the network on demand.

🧠

EchoFlow Adaptive Review

Intelligently schedules review opportunities based on continuous mastery and cooling protection, presenting phrases right when reinforcement is most effective.

🎧

Speech Input & Natural Audio

Converts spoken phrases into text via system speech recognition, pairing your practice with native device or app-level text-to-speech engines.

🌐

Strict Language Isolation

Supports 18 canonical languages with dedicated partitions. Libraries, review queues, and progress statistics never cross-contaminate across learning languages.

📦

Open Data Portability

Full JSON backups and standardized CSV imports allow you to export, archive, and transfer your study materials smoothly between Android and iOS without vendor lock-in.

⚡

Modern Native Craft

Built with Jetpack Compose (Android) and SwiftUI (iOS) for fluid performance, low resource footprint, and seamless platform interaction.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Transparent, Reliable, and User-Respecting

PhraseEcho does not operate proprietary user accounts, advertising networks, usage tracking, or centralized cloud upload servers. When you choose to enable optional third-party integrations, data is processed strictly according to our transparent privacy policy.
